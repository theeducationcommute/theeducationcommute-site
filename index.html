Welcome to The Education Commute—turning the research you don't have time to read into your 20-minute journey to work.

Each week, we take one piece of evidence—from UK benchmarks and EEF toolkits to global education research—and test how it actually translates to Monday morning reality in primary, secondary, and specialist classrooms worldwide.

We debate the nuance rather than reading the press release. Grounded in UK practice, built for educators everywhere, and fully sourced before the morning bell rings.

Hosted by a serving school leader working through the same NPQ frameworks the show covers — NPQH and NPQLBC completed, NPQSENCo in progress.

How we look after your email

What we collect. Your email address, when you subscribed, and whether you open or click our emails. Nothing else — no name, no school, no account.

Why. To send you one email each time a new episode goes live, with links to that week’s staffroom and parent one-pagers, and to unlock the full one-pager archive on this site. We don’t sell, rent or share the list with anyone, including sponsors.

Where it lives. With Kit (kit.com), the email service we use, under their privacy policy. This website itself stores nothing about you beyond a cookie on your own device that remembers you’ve subscribed.

Your choices. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe, which removes you immediately. To see, correct or delete what we hold, email newsletter@theeducationcommute.co.uk and we’ll do it within a few days.

Who’s responsible. The Education Commute is an independent podcast run by a serving practitioner in England; the person behind it is the data controller for this list. Legal basis: your consent, given when you confirm your subscription.
